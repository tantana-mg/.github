## Tantana in short

Tantana is HR and accounting for businesses in Madagascar, in one app:

- **People**: employees, contracts, teams and the attendance sheet.
- **Payroll**: payslips computed from attendance, with meal and transport
  allowances and lateness rules applied automatically.
- **Money**: monthly bookkeeping, treasury, taxes, expense claims and inventory.
- **Paperwork**: every payslip, note and report exported as a PDF, ready for the accountant.
- **Several companies** in one Tantana, each with its own data.
