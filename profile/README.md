<h1 align="center">Tantana</h1>

<h3 align="center">HR and accounting for businesses in Madagascar.</h3>

<p align="center">
  Your team, your payroll and your books in one app, with the local rules built in.
</p>

## What Tantana is

Tantana runs the back office of a small or medium Malagasy business:

| People | Money |
| --- | --- |
| Employees, contracts and team | Monthly bookkeeping with its documents |
| Attendance sheet and lateness | Treasury and taxes |
| Payslips computed from attendance | Expense claims and receipts |
| Adjustments and regularisations | Inventory |

Every payslip, note and report exports as a clean PDF, and one Tantana can
host several companies, each with its own data.

## What it solves

Most businesses here still run payroll and accounting on spreadsheets,
paper and memory, and the local rules (meal and transport allowances,
lateness withheld from them) are applied by hand. Tantana computes it all
from the attendance sheet, shows the reason behind every amount, and keeps
the paperwork ready for the accountant.
