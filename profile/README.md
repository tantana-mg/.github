<h1 align="center">Tantana</h1>

<p align="center">HR and accounting for small businesses in Madagascar.</p>

## What it is

One app for staff and the books: employees and contracts, attendance,
payslips calculated from the presence sheet, and monthly accounting with
its documents, all exported as PDF.

## What it solves

Small Malagasy businesses often run payroll and bookkeeping on spreadsheets
and paper. Tantana keeps it in one place, with the local rules (lateness,
meal and transport allowances) applied automatically.
